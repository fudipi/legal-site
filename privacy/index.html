Privacy Policy

Effective October 28, 2026

Your private library

Fudipi stores the photos you choose, card details, album pages, notes, local material unlocks, purchase delivery records and exported images in the app storage on your device. No account is required. The app does not upload your albums to our servers.

Photos and camera

We ask for permission when you choose photos, capture a card with the camera, or save exported images. Imported images are copied into app storage. Fudipi does not overwrite your original photos. You can change permissions in iOS Settings.

Purchases

Apple processes payments. Fudipi uses verified transaction identifiers and product identifiers to deliver purchased Credits once. Payment card information is not received by Fudipi. Local receipt records prevent repeated delivery after clearing content.

Sharing and third parties

We do not send your album photos or notes to a third-party processing service. If you choose Share, the destination you select receives the exported images with your permission. Apple services process purchase and permission requests under their own policies. Opening the policy website connects to its hosting provider.

Notifications

Notification permission is optional and can be changed in Settings. No advertising tracking is used.

Retention and deletion

Content remains on the device until you delete it or clear app data. Clear All App Data removes albums, imported copies, exports, unused Credits and local unlocks. Receipt tombstones are retained only to prevent duplicate grants. Original photos in Photos are not removed. Uninstalling the app can remove local content and entitlements. Keep exported copies.
